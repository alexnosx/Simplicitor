# Simplicitor v2.3.1 release notes

## What's fixed

- **Edit document:** the area where you drop a Word file now shows its full dashed outline at every Windows display scale.
- **Pull information from documents into Excel:** the Browse files button now sits directly under the box that lists your files.
- **Edit document:** drag across paragraphs to select a range; Shift-click still works.
- **Edit document:** when the local AI rewrites most of a paragraph, the new text takes one consistent format (that of most of the original paragraph) instead of mixing sizes or fonts from different parts of it.

Your documents and the AI processing stay on your computer; nothing is uploaded.

## Good to know

- Describe the change as an instruction, for example "Replace the text with: ...". Depending on the model, a statement on its own, such as "Batman is better", may be added as a note instead of replacing the text.

## Before you start

- You need Windows and Ollama running locally with a downloaded model. We recommend a model with at least 8 billion parameters (8B+) and a GPU with 8 GB of video memory.
- If you have Simplicitor 2.1.0, 2.2.0 or 2.3.0, the new installer replaces it; your settings and documents stay. If you installed 2.0.0 with Simplicitor-setup.exe, uninstall it from Settings > Apps first.
- The installer is unsigned. Windows may show an unknown-publisher or security warning.

## Downloads

- `Simplicitor-2.3.1.msi`: installer.
- `Simplicitor-portable.zip`: unpack and run without installing.
- `SHA256SUMS.json`: file checksums for verifying the downloads.
